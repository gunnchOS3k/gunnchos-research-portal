# 7GC, Research, and 6G Doctrine

## Role

7GC is the global validation/research network of the ecosystem.

It connects:
- 6G;
- NTN;
- AI-RAN;
- edge;
- digital twins;
- service continuity;
- security;
- measurement;
- digital equity;
- device/application workloads.

## Philosophy
Research should connect models to real user experience.

Wireless research is not only:
- throughput;
- latency;
- spectral efficiency.

It also affects:
- learning continuity;
- device usefulness;
- collaborative work;
- immersive interaction;
- access;
- reliability.

## Evidence ladder

```text
theory
→ simulation
→ synthetic evaluation
→ digital twin
→ emulation
→ prototype
→ controlled measurement
→ field measurement
→ operator integration
→ carrier/production validation
```

Every result must state which rung it occupies.

## Current research surfaces
- 7GC Digital Twin
- AI-RAN Field Kit
- ReadyGary beam selection
- SpectrumX AI-RAN Gary
- NTN resilience simulation
- Edge IO measurement node
- research portal

## Future state
A global validation fabric capable of connecting device, application, AI, network, digital-twin, and real-world measurements.

## Digital equity
Gary/community use cases are not side demonstrations.

They are a test of whether advanced networking becomes useful to communities that historically receive advanced infrastructure late.
