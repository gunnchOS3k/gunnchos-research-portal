# Anime Aggressors Creative and Gameplay Doctrine

## Identity

Anime Aggressors is a platform-fighter experience built around distinct power identities rather than generic recolors.

## House style
- anime character design;
- 3D cel-shaded presentation;
- 2D-like key-pose timing;
- platform-fighter readability;
- large expressive impact;
- cartoon squash/stretch;
- readable silhouettes;
- VFX that reinforces motion rather than hides weak motion.

## Fighter identities
- Ember — combustion
- Rook — mass
- Juno — current
- Kaia — flow
- Nix — structure
- Orion — vectors
- Vesper — uncertainty
- Yin — reduction
- Yang — definition

## Body presentation doctrine
Male/female forms represent the same identity.

They must share:
- personality;
- gameplay;
- move set;
- animation authority;
- hitboxes/hurtboxes;
- frame data;
- balance.

They may differ in authored presentation.

## Story-state doctrine
Spectrum fighters may move through:
- Normal
- Black Puppet
- White Puppet
- Essence 0 → 1 → 2 → 4 → 6
- Prismatic Gray

These must be visible states, not only data flags.

## Yin
Yin represents reduction:
- quiet;
- collapse;
- subtraction;
- folding;
- toward zero.

## Yang
Yang represents definition:
- radiance;
- construction;
- alignment;
- imposed order;
- toward infinity.

## Combat feel doctrine
Attack grammar:

```text
INTENT
→ ANTICIPATION
→ ACCELERATION
→ CONTACT
→ FOLLOW-THROUGH
→ RECOVERY
```

Do not hide weak pose design under particles.
