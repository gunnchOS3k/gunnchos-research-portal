# Accessibility, Digital Equity, and Education Doctrine

## Audience

The ecosystem should be usable and understandable by:
- children;
- seniors;
- non-technical adults;
- students;
- professionals;
- researchers;
- developers;
- people learning technology for the first time.

## Digital equity doctrine
Equity is not only cheaper hardware.

It includes:
- affordable access;
- offline capability;
- understandable documentation;
- repairability/maintainability goals;
- low barriers to learning;
- community relevance;
- language/readability;
- assistive technology;
- reasonable bandwidth requirements.

## Education doctrine
Every major system should be teachable.

The ecosystem should produce:
- diagrams;
- explanations;
- labs;
- source traceability;
- inspectable architecture;
- glossaries;
- examples.

## Accessibility doctrine
Automation may find issues.

Human lived experience remains required.

Do not treat passing an accessibility scanner as proof of human accessibility.

## Current state
Accessibility and human review are tracked separately in release gates.

## Future state
An ecosystem that helps users gain technical confidence rather than merely consume technology.
