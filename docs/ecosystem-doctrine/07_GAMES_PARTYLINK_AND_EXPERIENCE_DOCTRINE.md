# Games, PartyLink, and Experience Doctrine

## Games' role in the ecosystem

Games are:
- entertainment products;
- social experiences;
- quality benchmarks;
- interaction labs;
- animation/game-feel experiments;
- technical education surfaces;
- cross-device validation environments.

They must feel like real games.

## PartyLink doctrine

> **Controllers are personal. The match is shared.**

The player's personal device may act as controller/interface while the match is presented on a shared display.

Canonical roles:
- OWNER
- DISPLAY_HOST
- PLAYER
- SPECTATOR

Target:
- 2–8 players where the game supports it;
- local/LAN first;
- internet relay later where validated;
- Arena View shows shared authoritative state.

## Game quality principles
- readable states;
- strong feedback;
- clear silhouettes;
- responsive input;
- real gameplay loops;
- no prototype-feeling debug overlays in normal UX;
- no hidden fallbacks presented as completion;
- human fun/feel review is mandatory for final claims.

## Rights
Rights-safe content modes must exist independently of future commercial licensing.
