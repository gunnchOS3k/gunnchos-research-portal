Edmund Gunn Jr. proposes an article-based Communications Engineering dissertation on resilience-aware service continuity in heterogeneous 6G-era networks. The object is a constrained-control problem: keep human-facing services above a minimum-useful threshold on four resource-constrained device classes when latency, blockage, compute placement, energy, or NTN intermittency would otherwise drop them, even if connectivity metrics look acceptable. Three papers cover (I) continuity profiles and benchmarks, (II) radio-aware twin-informed cross-layer orchestration versus transparent baselines, and (III) terrestrial/edge/peer/offline/NTN fallback decision boundaries. The experimental stack is implemented and digitally exercisable; independent reproduction, physical RF/lab measurement, and any University of Oulu affiliation remain unclaimed.
