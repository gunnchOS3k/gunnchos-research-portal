# Three research questions

Canonical wording from *Edmund Gunn — Oulu Research Plan V3* (application submission). These are the primary RQs; shorter engineering interpretations live in [RQ_TO_REPO_EVIDENCE_MAP.md](../RQ_TO_REPO_EVIDENCE_MAP.md) and must not replace this wording.

1. **RQ1 / Paper I.** How can representative workloads and the constraints of four resource-constrained device classes be translated into measurable service-continuity profiles, metrics, and benchmark scenarios?

2. **RQ2 / Paper II.** To what extent can joint access selection, computation placement, fidelity/model adaptation, caching/checkpointing, and recovery control - informed by radio-aware digital-twin state and uncertainty - improve service-continuity utility under mobility, blockage, congestion, edge-resource variation, and energy constraints?

3. **RQ3 / Paper III.** Under which disruption conditions do terrestrial, local-edge, peer/device-to-device, offline, and NTN fallback modes preserve minimum useful service, and what performance, energy, privacy, and recovery tradeoffs arise in simulation, emulation, and device-level measurements?
