# PhD / Research Advisor path — gunnchOS Ecosystem v1.0.0-rc.1

## Research framing

gunnchOS is a reproducible, evidence-bounded platform for studying **6G / IMT-2030-class** problems: AI-assisted resource management, NTN resilience, service continuity, edge intelligence, AI-RAN, digital twins, user-centric QoE, and device/workload coupling.

## Evidence boundaries (honest)

- Accepted-main SHAs are the reproducibility pins.
- Research outputs may mix **synthetic** and **measured** inputs — each repo’s RESEARCH packet states which.
- Hardware is **digital engineering** for V1 RC1 — physical EVT/DVT/PVT and certification remain pending.
